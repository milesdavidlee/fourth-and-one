01 / Approach

Leveraging the power of athletes to impact consumer brands.

We invest in consumer brands and consumer tech companies Seed to Series B.

4th & 1 Ventures is a venture syndicate group that harnesses the influence and investment of top athletes, entertainers, and influencers along with the experience of seasoned business executives. With our expertise and extensive network, we serve as a trusted resource to our portfolio companies to open up doors and opportunities.

02 / Investment Approach

Unique approach to Investment

After the selected founders have undergone our extensive due diligence process, we invite the company to our quarterly virtual Shark Tank, where the team has the opportunity to pitch in real time to 4th & 1 investors.
